Every year or two, someone writes a headline that says "SEO is dead." It happened when social media took off. It happened when voice assistants were going to change everything. It's happening again now with AI.

I don't think SEO is dead. At Sublime Plus Marketing, we think it moved. And if you only look where it used to be, it can certainly look like it's gone.

## Why people keep saying it

The fear is understandable. When Google shows an AI Overview at the top of the page, fewer people scroll down to the links underneath.

[Pew Research Center looked at](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) real browsing data from March 2025 and found that people clicked a traditional search result in 8% of visits when an AI summary appeared, compared with 15% when it didn't. If your whole strategy depends on that click, those numbers are scary.

Add ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, which often answer without a list of links at all, and you can see why the "SEO is dead" crowd is loud again.

## What's still true

Here's what hasn't changed. Google still needs to crawl and understand your website. AI Overviews pull from Google's index. AI tools that browse the web still need clear, trustworthy pages to read.

A fast site, clear pages that answer real questions and genuine authority in your niche still matter. Maybe more than ever, because AI tools are picky about what they quote.

## What moved

Three big things moved.

**The goal moved from ranking to being named.** Ranking third on Google used to be the prize. Now the prize is being one of the brands mentioned inside the answer.

**The work moved from your site to the whole web.** AI tools lean heavily on what other people say about you, on Reddit, review sites and comparison articles. Your website is the foundation, but it's no longer the whole house.

**Measurement moved too.** You can't track an AI mention the way you track a ranking. You have to go ask the tools.

I broke down these differences in [SEO vs LLM optimization](/blog/seo-vs-llm-optimization-what-s-the-difference).

## So what do you call it now?

People call the new part different things. GEO, AEO, AI search optimization, LLM marketing. The labels keep changing, but the idea is the same: making sure your brand shows up where people now get their answers. I explained it plainly in [what is LLM marketing](/blog/what-is-llm-marketing).

## My honest take

SEO isn't dying. It's growing up. The brands that treat it as one part of a bigger picture, alongside reviews, community conversations and AI visibility, will keep winning. The brands that only chase rankings will keep reading "SEO is dead" headlines and wondering if they're true.
