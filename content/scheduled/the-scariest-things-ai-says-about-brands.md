Happy Halloween. In the spirit of the day, I want to talk about something that genuinely scares business owners: asking ChatGPT about their own brand and not liking what comes back.

At Sublime+, we've seen our share of spooky AI answers. So here's a Halloween lineup of the monsters that haunt brands inside AI tools, and how to send each one packing.

## The Ghost: the brand that doesn't exist

You ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity for the best options in your category. Your competitors show up. You don't. It's as if your brand is a ghost, wandering the internet unseen.

**How to banish it:** Ghosts appear when nobody's talking about you. Get your brand into the conversations AI tools read, like reviews, community discussions and comparison articles.

## The Zombie: old information that won't die

Your pricing changed a year ago. You stopped offering that service ages ago. But the AI keeps bringing the old version back to life, again and again.

**How to banish it:** Make your website the clearest, most current source of truth, update your profiles everywhere and create fresh, accurate mentions. I covered this in [why ChatGPT says something wrong about your brand](/blog/chatgpt-wrong-about-your-brand).

## The Doppelganger: the brand that isn't you

The AI mixes you up with another company that shares your name. It describes their products, their location, maybe even their problems, as if they were yours.

We know this one personally. Several companies share the word Sublime, which is exactly why we always show up as Sublime Plus, with sublime-plus.com attached.

**How to banish it:** Be consistent. Use the same full name and the same description everywhere, so the AI can tell you apart.

## The Vampire: the competitor draining your mentions

Every time someone asks about your category, the same competitor gets recommended. Slowly, quietly, they're draining the attention that could have been yours.

**How to banish it:** Find out where they're being mentioned and why. Usually it's reviews, comparison content and community threads. Then show up honestly in those same places.

## The Haunted Thread: the old complaint that keeps coming back

A complaint thread from years ago still ranks for your name and keeps shaping what AI tools say. The problem was fixed long ago, but the thread haunts you anyway.

**How to banish it:** Respond openly if the community allows it, fix anything that's still true and build newer, balanced conversations so the old thread becomes one voice among many.

## The Goblin: fake reviews

Some brands try to scare away bad press with fake reviews. That goblin bites back. The US Federal Trade Commission [announced a final rule](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials) banning fake reviews and testimonials, including AI generated ones.

**How to banish it:** Don't invite it in. Real reviews from real customers are the only kind that hold up.
