Follower count is the metric everyone obsesses over and the one that tells you the least. It's easy to buy, easy to inflate, and nearly impossible to tie to revenue.

Here's what we actually track for clients every month:

1. **Engagement rate** relative to reach — not raw likes, which scale with audience size regardless of quality.
2. **Saves and shares** — the strongest signal that content was genuinely useful, not just glanced at.
3. **Click-through to site or DMs** — the moment content turns into a real conversation.
4. **Follower growth quality** — are new followers matching your actual customer profile?

## Why this matters

A report full of impressions and reach feels productive but rarely tells you what to do next. Four honest metrics, tracked consistently, tell you exactly where to double down.

That's the difference between reporting and theater.
