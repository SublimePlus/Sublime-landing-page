Open ten competitor Instagram captions side by side and you'll notice something uncomfortable: they all sound the same. Same exclamation points, same "here's why," same safe, friendly-but-forgettable tone.

A real brand voice is specific. It has opinions, quirks, and a rhythm that's recognizable even without a logo attached.

## Where voice actually comes from

Voice isn't a style guide adjective like "playful" or "bold." It comes from real specifics: how your founder actually talks, what your best customers say back to you, the words your industry uses that outsiders don't.

## Our process

Before we write a single caption, we sit with founders, read support tickets, and listen to sales calls. The voice is already there — our job is finding it, not inventing it.
