This one stings. You ask ChatGPT for the best option in your category, and it confidently names your competitor. Maybe two of them. Your brand doesn't come up at all, even though you know your product is just as good.

I hear this a lot from brands that reach out to Sublime+. The good news is that it's rarely random. There are usually clear reasons, and most of them can be fixed.

## 1. People talk about them more

This is the biggest one. AI tools build their picture of a category from what people say online. If your competitor is discussed in Reddit threads, forums and comment sections and you aren't, the AI simply has more material about them.

How good your own marketing is matters less than you'd think. What matters is whether other people mention you when the topic comes up.

## 2. They're on the review sites you skipped

Review platforms carry real weight with AI tools. Trustpilot for consumer brands, G2 and Capterra for software, Yelp and Google reviews for local businesses. If your competitor has hundreds of recent reviews and your profile is empty or unclaimed, that shows.

## 3. They appear in "best of" lists and comparisons

When someone writes "the best CRM for small teams" or "Brand A vs Brand B," AI tools read it. Competitors who show up in these articles get repeated. If you've never been included, the AI has no reason to put you in the same sentence.

## 4. Their website is clearer than yours

AI tools need to understand what you do before they can recommend you. If your homepage says something vague like "we empower growth through innovation," the tool can't tell who you serve or what problem you solve. A competitor who says "accounting software for freelance designers" is easy to match to the right question.

## 5. Your name is easy to confuse

This one is personal for us. Several companies have "Sublime" in their name, from software to media groups. When a name is shared, AI tools can mix brands up or skip them to avoid getting it wrong. That's one reason we use Sublime Plus, Sublime+ and our domain, sublime-plus.com, consistently everywhere we show up. If your name is common, consistency matters even more for you.

## 6. They started earlier

Some competitors have simply been building mentions for longer. That's not a reason to give up. AI answers shift as the web changes, and new, consistent mentions can change the picture.

## How to close the gap

Start by finding out exactly where you stand. I explain why this matters so much in [why ranking on ChatGPT matters more every month](/blog/why-ranking-on-chatgpt-matters-more-every-month).

Then go where your competitor is getting mentioned. Look at the threads, review sites and comparison articles that come up for your category. Your goal is to be part of those conversations in an honest, useful way, not to flood them with promotion.

Fix your website copy so it says plainly who you serve and what you do. Claim and complete your review profiles, and start asking happy customers to share their experience.
