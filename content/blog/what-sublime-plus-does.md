I get asked some version of the same question almost every week: "So what does Sublime actually do?" Sometimes it comes from a founder who stumbled on one of our Reddit threads. Sometimes it's a marketing lead who heard the name on a call and wanted to understand us before booking time.

I figured it was time to write the full answer down in one place.

The short version is that **Sublime+ helps brands get found.** On Google, on Reddit, and more and more inside the answers that ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews give people when they ask for a recommendation. We do that through two things: organic Reddit content and AI generated visual content.

The longer version is below.

## Why I started Sublime

We started Sublime Plus in early 2026 because the way people discover products had changed faster than most marketing plans had.

Think about the last time you bought something you actually cared about. Running shoes, a CRM, a dentist in a new city. There's a good chance you typed your question into Google and added "reddit" at the end. Or you skipped Google entirely and asked ChatGPT.

Both habits lead to the same place. People trust other people more than they trust ads, and AI tools have learned to lean on the same sources people trust. Reddit threads show up all over Google results now, and they get pulled into AI answers constantly.

Most of the brands we spoke to already sensed this. Very few had a plan for it. That gap is the reason Sublime exists.

## How discovery works now

Classic SEO was mostly about your own website. You picked keywords, wrote pages, earned backlinks and climbed the rankings. That still matters, and we still care about it.

But a large language model doesn't only read your website when it decides what to recommend. It reads what other people say about you across the web. Forum threads, comparison posts, honest reviews, questions and replies. If your brand never comes up in those conversations, you're invisible to the tool your customer is asking.

That's what I mean when I talk about **LLM visibility**. It comes down to one question: when someone asks ChatGPT "what's the best option for my situation," does your name come up? The most reliable way to earn that mention is to show up, helpfully and consistently, in the places those models learn from.

Reddit is one of the biggest of those places.

## Organic Reddit content

This is the core of what Sublime Plus does. We write and publish Reddit posts and comments that answer real questions in the communities where your buyers already spend their time.

Organic is the important word here. We don't buy ads and we don't drop links into random threads. Every post is written to be genuinely useful to whoever is reading it, because Reddit users can smell a sales pitch from a mile away, and moderators remove anything that feels like one. A removed post helps nobody.

Our writers work from a detailed internal rulebook that covers tone, formatting, subreddit rules and what makes a comment read like it came from someone with real experience. We also handle the slow parts most brands don't have time for: finding the subreddits that matter in your niche, planning where and when to post, and staying inside each community's rules.

Every post we publish does two jobs at once. It helps the person asking the question today, and it becomes a page that Google indexes and AI models read later. One good thread can keep sending traffic and mentions long after it goes live.

## AI generated visual content

The second half of Sublime+ is visual. Brands need a steady stream of images and video, and most can't afford a photo shoot every month.

We produce four kinds of visual content:

**Faceless content** for brands that want to teach, explain or entertain without putting a person on camera.

**AI brand ambassadors**, consistent characters who represent your brand across posts and videos with no scheduling, travel or talent fees.

**AI influencers** built to grow an audience in a specific niche and carry your message to it.

**Product imagery** that shows the same product in dozens of settings without shipping a single unit anywhere. Ecommerce brands love this one.

Real customer stories still matter most. What this changes is that you're never stuck waiting on a shoot before you can post something good.

## Why the two work together

A Reddit thread builds trust. A strong visual earns attention. When someone sees your brand in a helpful thread, then again in their feed, then hears about it from ChatGPT, your name starts to feel familiar. Familiar brands get clicked, and familiar brands get bought.

That repetition across channels sits at the center of how we think about growth at Sublime.

## Who we work with

Sublime Plus works with three kinds of clients.

**B2B companies** with long sales cycles. Their buyers research quietly for weeks, and a lot of that research now happens on Reddit and inside AI chats.

**Ecommerce brands** that need to show up when shoppers ask "is this worth it" or "what should I buy instead."

**Local businesses** that want to be the name people hear when someone in their city asks for a recommendation.

Our clients are spread across the USA, Canada, South America and Europe. The team is fully remote, and each person owns one part of the process, from research and writing to posting and visual production. Nobody is juggling five roles at once, and the quality shows it.
